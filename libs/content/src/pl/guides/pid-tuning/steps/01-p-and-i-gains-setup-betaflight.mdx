1. Connect your quad to Betaflight and go into the PID Tuning tab (#1).
2. Enable Expert Mode in the top right of the Configurator (#2)
3. Move the "Drift / Wobble" (I Gain) Slider down to 0 (#3)
4. Move the "Stick Response - FF Gains" Slider (#4) to 0
5. Hit "Save"
